# SelectiCore Four-test School Readiness Assessment

The School Estimator is available from the home page, dashboard, Readiness page and Test Centre.

It runs one generated four-section mock in this order:
1. Reading — 45 minutes
2. Mathematical Reasoning — 40 minutes
3. Thinking Skills — 40 minutes
4. Writing — 30 minutes

At the end, it shows each section result and an equal-weight practice average. The Writing score is generated by SelectiCore's automated writing practice rubric; it is not an official NSW examiner mark.

## Important limitation
This is a practice and target-planning aid, not an admissions predictor. The percentage is not an official NSW scaled placement score; the tool does not use a validated relationship between SelectiCore results and school offers or current school cut-offs. It must not claim that a student is guaranteed admission or that a specific school is the most likely outcome. It provides cautious target-research suggestions and encourages students to review results across multiple mocks.
